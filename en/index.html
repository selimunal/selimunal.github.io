About

Assistant Professor at Kastamonu University, Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering. Working in computational materials science, combining first-principles (DFT) calculations with machine learning and deep learning to understand and predict the properties of nanomaterials, with a focus on optoelectronic and sensing applications.

Research Areas

📈 Time Series Analysis

AI-based prediction models for complex time series data. Expertise in LSTM, ARIMA, and hybrid models with advanced machine learning techniques.

View Publication

⚛️ Nanomaterials

Machine learning for nanomaterial property prediction and optimization. Advanced algorithms for material characterization and performance enhancement.

💡 Optoelectronics

Research on photodetectors and polymer-based devices. Innovative approaches to enhance optoelectronic device performance using cutting-edge technologies.

Selected Publication

  • Fast and Highly Sensitive Ionic-Polymer-Gated WS2–Graphene Photodetectors

    J. D. Mehew, S. Unal, E. Torres Alonso, G. F. Jones, S. Fadhil Ramadhan, M. F. Craciun, S. Russo
